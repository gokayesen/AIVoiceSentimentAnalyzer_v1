## What Went Well

- The import-path-string RQ enqueue pattern established in Story 1.2 kept `web-api` and `ml-service` decoupled (AD-7) and was reused unchanged through Story 1.9.
- Story 1.6's five-site "fan-in" enqueue design (fusion always runs regardless of the transcript branch's outcome) — called out as the epic's single most architecturally significant decision.
- Story 1.3's "compute in memory, write once" atomicity discipline, adopted epic-wide after being the first place partial-write bugs were caught.
- The "previous story intelligence" pattern in each story file visibly reduced repeat review findings over time (e.g. Story 1.5 built in a fix from the start that Story 1.4 only got via review).
- Honest, unflattering reporting culture: Story 1.3's real (n=4) CREMA-D spot-check was reported as baseline-level, not spun as a success.

## What Was Challenging

- **Platform friction:** Intel Mac (x86_64) had no native PyTorch wheel, forcing a Docker-based workflow for every `ml-service` story (1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6, 1.9) while `web-api` ran natively (1.1, 1.7, 1.8, 1.10) — a recurring context-switch cost, not a code-quality problem per se.
- **Model/library assumptions breaking in practice:** Story 1.3's planned SER checkpoint's classifier head silently failed to load (randomly initialized weights); Story 1.5's planned model turned out unlicensed and was swapped; Story 1.4 discovered an undocumented `torchcodec`+ffmpeg dependency. Recurring lesson: documentation claims are not a substitute for empirical verification.
- **Recurring code-review findings (systemic, not one-off):** `db.get_connection()` calls sitting outside try/except (5 instances across ingest/acoustic/transcript/text_sentiment/fusion); `PRAGMA foreign_keys` never enabled; no idempotency/upsert guard against RQ retries; confidence values never validated finite/non-NaN before arithmetic.

## Key Insight

"Documentation says it works" is not the same as "verified in the actual environment." This gap caused every major model/library surprise in the epic and should become an explicit process step (empirical preflight) rather than something each story rediscovers independently.
