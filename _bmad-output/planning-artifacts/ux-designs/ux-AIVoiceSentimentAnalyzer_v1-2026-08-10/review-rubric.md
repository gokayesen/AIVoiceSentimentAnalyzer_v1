# Spine Pair Review — AI Voice Sentiment Analyzer

## Overall verdict

The spine pair is disciplined and largely source-faithful: every citation to the PRD/Brief/Technical Research checked out verbatim, all three `sources` frontmatter entries resolve to real files, and there is no meaningful bloat or scope creep. It falls short of a clean contract in three concrete places: one frontmatter color token (`text-faint`) fails WCAG AA contrast against its own stated backgrounds despite governing a "standing, never-dismissible" compliance disclaimer; the delete-confirmation interaction and the FR-11 dual-signal panel are named repeatedly but never given an owning component spec in either file; and normal (non-delete) navigation back from the Analysis Dashboard to the Session Call List is never specified. None of these are blocking rewrites, but each would force a downstream architect or dev to invent a decision the spine should have made.

## 1. Flow coverage — strong
PRD §2.3 defines exactly one Key User Journey (UJ-1); no other UJs or named requirements-as-flows exist in the sources. EXPERIENCE.md § Key Flows reproduces UJ-1 verbatim (title matches PRD word-for-word), with named protagonist (Elif), numbered steps 1–4, an explicit Climax and Resolution beat, and an edge case (the disagreement scenario at 04:05).
### Findings
- **low** The Key Flow's only alternate branch is the disagreement "edge case" — there is no failure branch inside the flow itself (e.g., upload rejected, processing fails mid-call), even though FR-2/FR-3 make failure a first-class part of realizing UJ-1 steps 1–2 (EXPERIENCE.md § Key Flows, UJ-1). Those failure states do exist, but only in § State Patterns, disconnected from the flow narrative. *Fix:* add one sentence to UJ-1's Path or a short "Failure" line (matching the calibration examples' pattern) pointing at the validation/processing-failure states so the flow is self-contained.

## 2. Token completeness — thin
All 22 color tokens, 7 typography roles, the `rounded`/`spacing` scales, and all 12 `components` entries in DESIGN.md frontmatter have concrete values; every `{path.to.token}` reference inside the `components` block and in body prose (e.g. `{rounded.sm}`, `{spacing.8}`, `{components.icon-button}`) resolves to a defined token — no dangling references found.
### Findings
- **critical** `text-faint` (`#8A939C`) fails WCAG AA contrast against both surfaces it's specified to sit on: ≈2.85:1 on `bg` (`#F3F5F7`) and ≈3.12:1 on `panel` (`#FFFFFF`), well under the 4.5:1 normal-text minimum. This token is the foreground for `disclaimer-bar` (DESIGN.md § Components, § frontmatter `components.disclaimer-bar`) — the "always present, never dismissible" standing disclaimer EXPERIENCE.md § Voice and Tone calls load-bearing for NFR-2/NFR-4 — and the default foreground for `icon-button` (`foreground-default: '{colors.text-faint}'`). Neither DESIGN.md nor EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor states a contrast target anywhere, so this wasn't caught by the spec itself. *Fix:* darken `text-faint` (e.g. toward `text-dim`'s `#5B6672`, which passes at ≈5.4:1) or restrict its use to large/non-text elements, and add an explicit contrast rule to DESIGN.md Do's and Don'ts or EXPERIENCE.md Accessibility Floor for the disclaimer/label gray-scale.
- **medium** No section in either file states contrast targets for any color pair, even though the rubric-relevant pairs are genuinely tight (e.g. `low-confidence` `#AEB6BE` border against `panel` is ≈2.05:1, under the 3:1 UI-component-boundary minimum — mitigated somewhat by the redundant hatch pattern, but never verified in writing). *Fix:* add one line to DESIGN.md § Colors or Do's and Don'ts asserting the WCAG level targeted, and spot-check the gray scale against it.

## 3. Component coverage — adequate
All 12 `components` frontmatter entries have a matching DESIGN.md § Components prose bullet. EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns gives real behavioral rules (not one-word) for Timeline, Summary cells, Transcript turn, Dual-signal panel, Acoustic metric bar, and Call row.
### Findings
- **high** The delete-confirmation step is required twice ("a confirmation step is required before deletion actually occurs" — EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns, Call row; § Interaction Primitives, Delete a Call) but no component anywhere — DESIGN.md § Components or frontmatter `components`, or EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns — specifies what it *is* (modal dialog, inline two-step button, toast). A downstream builder has nothing to implement against. *Fix:* add a `confirm-dialog` (or equivalent) entry to DESIGN.md `components` + Components prose, and a Component Patterns row describing its trigger/dismiss behavior.
- **medium** "Dual-signal panel" is named and given real behavioral rules in EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns (the canonical FR-11 rendering), but DESIGN.md never gives it an owning row — it's only a parenthetical inside the `transcript-turn` bullet ("a `dual` two-up sub-panel showing the text-signal reading and tone-signal reading side by side"). Given this is explicitly the mechanism that keeps a PRD-load-bearing requirement (FR-11, "never silently resolved") visually legible, it deserves its own visual spec (layout, spacing between the two halves, label typography) rather than a clause inside another component. *Fix:* promote `dual-signal-panel` to its own `components` frontmatter entry and DESIGN.md Components bullet.
- **medium** `badge-dot` has a frontmatter token spec (size 7px, `rounded.full`) and one inline mention in DESIGN.md's Call row bullet ("sentiment/emotion with `badge-dot`"), but no dedicated DESIGN.md Components bullet explaining its color mapping (presumably the same `negative`/`mixed`/`positive`/`neutral-signal` variants as `timeline-segment`, but this is never stated), and it doesn't appear anywhere in EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns. *Fix:* give `badge-dot` its own short bullet in DESIGN.md stating its color-variant mapping, and mention it explicitly in EXPERIENCE.md's Call row pattern.

## 4. State coverage — adequate
Session Call List states (empty, uploading/validating, processing, processing failed, complete, deleting) and Analysis Dashboard states (low-confidence segment, disagreement segment, speaker attribution unavailable, speaker attribution uncertain) are all covered in EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns with real, distinguishing rules.
### Findings
- **high** There is no specified mechanism for the *normal* (non-delete) case of returning from the Analysis Dashboard to the Session Call List. UJ-1 step 4 says Elif "moves on, selecting the next Call in the Session Call List," implying she navigates back, but no State Pattern, Component Pattern, or Interaction Primitive says how — the only stated return path is the delete-triggered one ("If the deleted Call was open in the Analysis Dashboard, the analyst returns to the Session Call List," § State Patterns, Deleting). DESIGN.md's `app-header` mentions "a monospace breadcrumb (queue/case path)" which is the plausible mechanism, but EXPERIENCE.md never states it's clickable or what it does. *Fix:* add a line to § Interaction Primitives or § Component Patterns (App header) specifying the return-to-list action.
- **low** The Summary cells' "Segments Flagged" cell is defined as "a count, itself a link/anchor to the first flagged segment" (§ Component Patterns), but its zero-count state isn't addressed — unlike the adjacent Secondary Signal cell, which explicitly gets a "None flagged" fallback in the same bullet. *Fix:* one clause covering Segments Flagged = 0 (e.g., "shows 0, not a link, when no segment is flagged").
- **medium** EXPERIENCE.md § Foundation promises the layout "should degrade gracefully rather than break on a narrower window," but no state or breakpoint behavior defines what "graceful" means for the two-column 60/40 transcript/acoustic grid or the four-cell summary row at narrower widths — see also Shape fit finding below.

## 5. Visual reference coverage — adequate
Only one visual reference file exists in the project (`.working/directions-1.html`); `mockups/`, `wireframes/`, and `imports/` are empty or absent. Per `.memlog.md`, this single file depicts both the Analysis Dashboard hero screen (low-confidence segment, disagreement state, speaker-attributed transcript, acoustic panel) and the paired Session Call List screen, across four rendered directions, of which Direction 1 was selected.
### Findings
- **medium** EXPERIENCE.md references the file exactly once, at the very end of § Key Flows ("→ Composition reference: `.working/directions-1.html` (Direction 1, selected). Spine wins on conflict with any mock."), rather than in § Information Architecture where the Session Call List and Analysis Dashboard surfaces are actually defined — the placement convention both calibration examples (`experience-example-mobile.md`, `experience-example-shadcn.md`) use. The reference also doesn't name what the file illustrates (no mention that it covers both surfaces, or that it's one of four rendered directions), just "(Direction 1, selected)." *Fix:* move or duplicate the composition reference into § Information Architecture, next to the surface table, and state what it depicts (both surfaces) per the established convention.
- **low** DESIGN.md never references `.working/directions-1.html` even though, per `.memlog.md`, its entire token set was extracted directly from that file ("First full draft of DESIGN.md (Clinical/Data-Dense tokens extracted from directions-1.html Direction 1)"). This matches the calibration precedent (neither example DESIGN.md links to a mockup) so it isn't a hard miss, but a one-line provenance note in § Brand & Style would help a reader verify the spec against its source.

## 6. Bloat & overspecification — strong
No pixel values duplicate what tokens already cover, no persona/FR/scope restatement beyond brief citations (e.g. "(§5, Open Question 7)"), and DESIGN.md's editorial voice stays proportionate while EXPERIENCE.md stays behavioral. No findings.

## 7. Inheritance discipline — adequate
All three `sources` frontmatter entries (PRD, Brief, Technical Research) resolve to real files. Every spot-checked FR/NFR/SM/UJ citation (FR-11, NFR-1 through NFR-5, SM-2, PRD §10, Technical Research §3.1/§3.3/§5.4/§8.3–§8.4) matches its source accurately. Component names are consistent between DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md for the six components both files spec in depth.
### Findings
- **low** EXPERIENCE.md's opening inheritance statement — "term usage (Call, Analyst, Sentiment, Emotion, Confidence, Low-Confidence Segment, Analysis Result, Human Review) follows the PRD Glossary exactly" — omits four PRD Glossary terms that are in fact used consistently throughout both spines: Acoustic Analysis, Transcript Analysis, Fusion, and Emotional Timeline. Usage is correct everywhere checked (e.g., IA table's "Emotional Timeline"), but the explicit list understates what's actually inherited, which could read as license to redefine those four terms downstream. *Fix:* extend the inherited-terms list to the full PRD Glossary set.
- **low** DESIGN.md's "Timeline segment" (the individual segment) and EXPERIENCE.md's "Timeline" (the overall strip) label the same underlying frontmatter token (`components.timeline-segment`) with two different English names. EXPERIENCE.md does cross-reference the token explicitly, so this reads as a deliberate whole-vs-part distinction rather than an error, but it's worth a mechanical note.

## 8. Shape fit — adequate
DESIGN.md's present sections (Brand & Style, Colors, Typography, Layout & Spacing, Shapes, Components, Do's and Don'ts) are in canonical order. EXPERIENCE.md has all eight required-by-default sections (Foundation, IA, Voice and Tone, Component Patterns, State Patterns, Interaction Primitives, Accessibility Floor, Key Flows).
### Findings
- **medium** DESIGN.md silently omits Elevation & Depth — it isn't between Layout & Spacing and Shapes, and nothing elsewhere states whether the product uses shadows/elevation at all. Both calibration DESIGN.md examples include this section even to say "we don't use elevation" (Quill: "Quill avoids elevation as a visual device..."; Drift: "Inherited from shadcn — subtle shadow on hover/active states..."). Given this product now needs at least one elevated surface (the delete-confirmation dialog flagged in Component coverage), the omission is no longer a neutral "not applicable" case. *Fix:* add a short Elevation & Depth section — even a two-sentence "flat by design, one exception for the confirmation dialog" would close the gap.
- **medium** EXPERIENCE.md never has a § Responsive & Platform section, yet § Foundation explicitly makes a responsive-behavior claim ("the layout should degrade gracefully rather than break on a narrower window") — the exact trigger condition the rubric's "required-when-applicable" list names for this section. The two calibration EXPERIENCE.md examples both include this section whenever such a claim is made (Drift's breakpoint table). *Fix:* either add a minimal Responsive & Platform section defining the degradation behavior for the two-column grid and summary row, or soften the Foundation claim to "desktop-only, no responsive behavior defined" if that's the actual intent.

## Mechanical notes
- Both DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md `sources` frontmatter resolve correctly to the PRD, Product Brief, and Technical Research files at their stated `{planning_artifacts}` paths.
- Both files carry `status: draft` — consistent with each other, but worth confirming this is the intended state before downstream consumption.
- No broken `{path.to.token}` cross-references found in either file.
- No orphaned visual-reference files: the only asset (`.working/directions-1.html`) is referenced (see Finding 5 above for placement/specificity issues).
- Component-name casing/pluralization is consistent enough to resolve unambiguously everywhere checked (e.g., `summary-cell` token vs. "Summary cells" prose label) except the `text-faint` contrast issue and the `badge-dot` / dual-signal-panel gaps already noted above.
