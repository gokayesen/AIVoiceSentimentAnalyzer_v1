- (event) Web research (landscape check) findings: (1) Acoustic+transcript fusion is standard practice at enterprise call-center analytics vendors (CallMiner, Verint, Cogito, Behavioral Signals, NICE) — cannot be claimed as a category-first differentiator. Honest differentiation is accessibility/transparency/technical-depth-as-portfolio-artifact, not concept novelty. (2) SER accuracy is highly dataset-dependent: acted/clean datasets (RAVDESS ~89-97%, CREMA-D ~82-90%) score far higher than naturalistic conversational datasets (IEMOCAP ~76%), and call-center audio resembles the harder IEMOCAP-like conditions, not the easy acted-dataset conditions. High accuracy claims should NOT be made in product docs. (3) Turkish SER has real but sparse/small academic datasets (BUEMDB, TurEV-DB) and no commercial tooling — Turkish support is a genuine open risk, not a strength to claim yet. (4) Similar open-source portfolio projects exist but mostly do transcript-only OR single-modality acoustic-only on acted datasets; genuine acoustic+NLP fusion on realistic 2-party conversational audio is comparatively rare and a legitimate differentiator.

(6) Hume AI (only dedicated cloud acoustic-emotion API) is sunsetting June 2026 - reinforces local/open-weight inference as better-evidenced default. (7) Recommended MVP shape: rule-based/late fusion with temperature-scaled confidence, UAR/macro-F1 evaluation against majority-class and single-modality baselines.
